# Welcome to the Applicaudia Blog

I build software, and this blog is where I write about how it works.

Applicaudia began as a consulting practice. Today, however, it is an independent app studio. I design and ship mobile apps, web apps, and tools for audio engineering, sports management, and party games.

Specifically, this space covers:

- **App Development:** How I build and ship production software.
- **Audio and Tuner Tech:** Signal processing, pitch detection, and strobe tuners.
- **Sports Scheduling:** Solver algorithms, automation, and real-world logistics.
- **Engineering Deep-dives:** Architecture, cross-platform development, and performance.
- **Build-in-Public:** Honest notes on what works, what breaks, and what ships next.

## What to Expect

Here, I write as a hands-on engineer sharing concrete implementations. So posts focus on technical challenges, architectural choices, and practical lessons from shipping real products.

For example, our newest series covers building [LoopCloser](https://loopcloserapp.com), an AI-assisted manager notebook. As a result, I am documenting the process as I enter it into RevenueCat's Ship-a-ton with a live store deadline.

Then, check back regularly or follow [@applicaudia on X](https://x.com/applicaudia) for updates.
